# Harness Sketch

Describe Juno's role and task: Juno reads a week’s raw signals (interviews, support tickets, emails, sales notes, Slack) and drafts a ranked priority list, with the evidence and source behind each item

_Rough first pass, Module 3 Lab 1. Not the AI PRD._

## 01 · Context
User interview transcripts, support tickets, executive emails, CRM sales notes and Slack engineering threads, plus the Product Strategy One-Pager to rank them against.

## 02 · Tools
It can group related signals across sources, filter out noise, rank priorities and flag conflicts between sources. It cannot reply to anyone, create or close tickets, post anywhere, or commit to dates.

## 03 · Loop
One pass per batch of signals. It flags anything ambiguous instead of guessing

## 04 · Memory
Nothing yet.

## 05 · Permissions
Draft the ranked list is automatic. Nothing is sent, posted or escalated without human review

## 06 · Verification
Nothing checks it automatically yet.
